# D minor, 128 bpm, 16 bars = 30 s. The cut is built on these bars (trailer/edit.txt):
#    1-2  cold open: drone, sonar pings, a heartbeat        ("Vampire, vampire, vampire!")
#    3-5  build: a hit as the gun opens up, taiko and string ostinato, Dm Bb C
#    6-7  riser: strings up the octave, a snare roll, Gm A, then a beat of nothing
#    8-10 the drop: braams, half-time drums, brass stabs, Dm Bb C
#   11-13 the gag: a record scratch and silence            (the mount and the Seahawk)
#   14    back in, harder: the title
#   15-16 the last hit, ringing out under the end card     ("And there goes my tax refund.")
